Questions, answered.

Do I keep ownership of the profile?

Yes. You remain the owner and can give us manager access. We never need your Google password.

Can you guarantee verification or a top Maps ranking?

No. Google determines how you verify and where a profile appears. We help with the setup and keep your details current.

Can you work on a profile I already have?

Yes. Start with management and we’ll include a basic tidy-up in the first month.

Do you also run my website or social pages?

This service covers Google Business Profile. Website work, Facebook posting and suspension recovery are separate.
